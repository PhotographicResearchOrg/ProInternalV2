## 1. Purpose — what this program does

This program closes the unreceived balance (backorder) on purchase order lines, either for every open line on a PO or for products the operator picks. A line is closed by setting its received quantity equal to its ordered quantity. The product's on-order tracking in IVMST (PRO positions 20–23) is updated, and the PO is flagged complete when every line is closed. It does NOT notify the vendor, record a cancellation reason, user or date, or touch member special orders.

## 7. Bug suspects

- **Message does not match the action (lines 154–157):** "CANCELLATION ABANDONED" is shown when QR is `END`/`E`, but the PO has already been written (line 154). In option 2, entering `E` at the per-line prompt just skips that line. Nothing is abandoned.
- **Whole-PO receipt test (line 128):** `PO(11)=''` tests the entire receipt-date attribute, not the line. Once any line on the PO has been received, the remaining open quantity of *every* product goes to BO (attr 23) instead of PO.QTY (attr 21).
- **Described lines mapped to 555 (line 101):** a real product with a description override updates 555 instead of the product, so that product's on-order tracking is never cleared.
- **Lost information (line 115):** after cancellation, received qty overstates actual receipts for any report that sums IVPO attr 9.
- **Unconditional rewrite (line 154):** the PO is written even when nothing was cancelled.
- **Meaningless assignment (line 58):** `TOT=PO(6)` assigns the product list to a total. Harmless.
- **No audit:** no user, date or reason for a cancelled backorder.
- READU silent-fallthrough: not present (`MATREADU M ... ELSE MAT M=''; M(4)='NOF'` is not-found only; no LOCKED clause, so it waits).

## 8. Schema-design notes for the CTO

- Modifies **PurchaseOrderLine** (closes the balance) and **Product on-order** tracking.
- Add a distinct **qtyCancelled** (with reason, user and date) to the PO line instead of overloading qtyReceived.
- On-order quantity should be derived from open PO lines, not stored as MV lists on IVMST (attrs 20–23).
- The split between "on PO" (attr 21) and "backordered" (attr 23) depends on whether any receipt happened. Decide whether the new model needs that distinction (it feeds availability and `ORDDUE`).
- A cancelled vendor backorder may need to trigger member special-order and backorder handling. Check `IV.ORD.REC` and `IV11.AUTO` first.
