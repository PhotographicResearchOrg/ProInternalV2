## 1. Purpose — what this program does

For a special-order line on a PO, this screen records **who the goods are for**: one or more customers (PRO members from ARMST, or retail/mailing-list customers from IVCUST), the quantity for each, and the extended sale. Total customer quantity must equal the PO line quantity. It can add new mailing-list customers (IVCUST + name/phone index). It writes the special-order record to IVSPORD and returns the total sale to the PO line.

**Reachability warning: read before classifying.** The only caller in BP is `IV46.PROD` label 710, and every `GOSUB 710` there is commented out (IV46.PROD lines 960–963). This routine is not executed by current code unless something outside BP calls it. The rules are extracted so the business can decide whether customer distribution of special orders is still needed in PIV2. The user's brief described this as "special-order POs, product 555". Attaching an existing special order via 555 is in `IV46.PROD` (IV46.PROD.07–.08), not here.

## 7. Bug suspects

- **Unreachable (IV46.PROD 960–963):** the only call site is commented out. Customer distribution has not been captured from PO entry since that change.
- **Exit check uses the wrong total (157, 161):** leaving an existing record compares cost with the input parameter `SALE` (not computed in this routine) instead of `TOT.ESALE`.
- **Yen cost breaks arithmetic (102, 106, 459, 467):** `UNIT.COST` may carry a `Y` suffix (IV46.PROD.21). Multiplying it gives wrong totals, and the MD2 display shows yen as dollars.
- **Unit vs extended ambiguity (544–558):** the same field is multiplied or not depending on its size, so a genuine low extended sale gets multiplied by qty.
- **Array overflow (10, 528–530):** more than 30 new customers in one session overflows `CUST.LIST`/`LNUM.LIST`.
- **Phone index edge case (130–132):** with `LENPH=0`, `PHONE[-3,4]` is evaluated.
- **IDATE argument mismatch (343):** 2 arguments passed to a 4-argument subroutine.
- **No lock on IVSPORD/IVCUST (126, 138):** concurrent edits overwrite each other.
- **READU/MATREADU silent-fallthrough:** not present.

## 8. Schema-design notes for the CTO

- Entities: Special Order (header: item, qty, unit cost, PO link), Special Order Allocation (customer, qty, sale, store), Retail Customer / Contact.
- Members and retail customers share one "customer" field (overloaded: member #, IVCUST id or raw name). PIV2 should have a typed reference (member vs retail contact).
- Sale is in cents and may be unit or extended. Store unit price and quantity, and derive extended.
- The `'O':SO#` backup records are a manual undo mechanism. Not needed with transactions.
- Missing audit: who created or changed the allocation and when.
- Decide first whether the process is alive: check IVSPORD for records with recent PO dates (attr 5) and customers (attr 7).
- Sibling extractions: `IV46.PROD` (attaches special orders, done), `IV52` (receiving distributes to special orders, done), and whatever creates IVSPORD records today (not found in this batch, so search BP for `WRITE ... ON IVSPORD`).
