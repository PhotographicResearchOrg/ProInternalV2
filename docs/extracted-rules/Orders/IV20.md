## 1. Purpose — what this program does

Prints (or shows on screen) a purchase order. The PO number and print flag come from the caller's DATA stack, and the list of products comes from a select list the caller must activate. Each product's quantity and unit cost are read from the product-side PO data (IVMST 21/22), not from IVPO. It prints a company header, one line per product and totals. It does NOT print vendor name/address, terms, PO date, special instructions or one-time-vendor details. `IV45` (extracted separately) appears to be the current PO print, and nothing in BP calls IV20.

## 7. Bug suspects

- **Mixed-currency grand total (39, 41, 46):** `GTOT` adds yen and cent amounts, and the label follows whichever line printed last.
- **Partly received POs print blank quantities (37):** once the PO has a receipt, open qty sits in IVMST 23, so lines print with no qty and zero cost.
- **Non-stock lines omitted (30):** special-order lines whose product is not in IVMST are skipped with "NOT ON FILE".
- **Letter PO numbers rejected at the prompt (13):** `MATCHES '0N'` refuses `A12345`/`12345B`, which IV.ORD.REC allows.
- **Stale company address (20–22):** Fairfield, CT; other programs use Hatboro, PA.
- **Wrong date (24, 26):** prints today's date, not the PO date (IVPO attr 2).
- **No vendor on the PO:** the printed PO has no vendor block, so it cannot be sent as-is.
- **READU/MATREADU silent-fallthrough:** not present (read-only).

## 8. Schema-design notes for the CTO

- Entity read: product-side PO lines. PIV2 PO documents must be generated from the PO header + lines (vendor, ship-to, terms, dates, currency).
- Company identity (legal name, address, phone) should be configuration, not code.
- Currency must be per PO (or per line with conversion) so totals are meaningful.
- Compare with `IV45` (current PO print, extracted) before deciding anything here; IV20 is probably OBSOLETE.
