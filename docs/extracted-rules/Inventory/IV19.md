## 1. Purpose — what this program does

An inquiry screen: the user enters a product and sees every open PO for it, read from the product-side PO multivalues in IVMST (PO number, a "date", quantity, unit cost, line total), with a total quantity. It is read-only. It does NOT read IVPO, show the vendor, or show backorder balances. It was written in 1983 for an older meaning of IVMST attr 23 (see §7).

## 7. Bug suspects

- **Backorder qty shown as a date (22, 24):** `OCONV(M(23),'D2.')` turns a backorder quantity like 12 into 01.12.68.
- **Outstanding understated (23, 33):** lines partly received show a blank qty and 0 total, because the balance is in attr 23.
- **Parallel lists (19–22):** the loop ends on attr 20's delimiter only. If attrs 21–23 are out of step, values shift between lines.
- **READU/MATREADU silent-fallthrough:** not present (read-only).

## 8. Schema-design notes for the CTO

- Entity read: product-side copy of PO lines. In PIV2 this becomes a query over PO lines (PO, vendor, PO date, ordered, received, open, unit cost, currency, expected date) on the product page, which is exactly the planned PIV2 feature.
- Confirms the historical overload of IVMST 23 (date → backorder qty). Profile IVMST 23 data before migration; old records may still hold dates.
- Sibling extractions: `IV.ORD.REC`, `IV46.PROD`, `BLD.IVPO` (done).
