## 1. Purpose — what this program does

A buyer runs this to calculate suggested purchase quantities for one product group (optionally one sub-group). For each product it compares this year's monthly sales with last year's, projects demand for the requested number of months, subtracts available stock, rounds the result, and writes the quantity to the `ORDER` work file. `IV.ORD.REC` option 2 / `IV.AUTO.ORDER` later turns that file into a PO. It prints its working for every product. It does NOT pick a vendor, create a PO, use the product's MOQ/case/carton/min-stock fields, or clear the `ORDER` file.

Note: `DH1` (1985) writes the same `ORDER` file with a **different formula**. A third formula exists only in the IVMST dictionary (`MIN.STK`/`Order` I-descriptors, see §8). Which one the buyers actually use today must be confirmed before PIV2 copies any of them.

## 7. Bug suspects

- **Dead code (36–37):** line 36 sets CMOS only to have line 37 overwrite it unconditionally. Effective rule is CMOS = min(MON, AMOS).
- **Divide by zero, end of year (10, 47):** from 20 Dec to 19 Jan MON = 12, so FMON = 0. Any product with last-year sales (PREV ≥ 1) divides by zero at line 47.
- **Divide by zero, low history (33, 44):** PAVG = INT(PREV/PMOS + .5) can be 0 when PREV ≥ 1 (e.g. PREV = 1 over 3 months), and line 44 divides by PAVG.
- **Divide by zero, pack size (61–62):** a blank attr 2 is defaulted to 10 but a zero is not, so `QUAN / R(2)` fails.
- **Backorder balance ignored (29):** IVMST 23 is not added to on order. After any partial receipt (`IV.ORD.REC.17` moves the whole balance to attr 23) the product looks un-ordered and is suggested again. The IVMST dictionary's own `ORDERED`/`ON-ORD` items do include attr 23.
- **Blank vs zero months (32, 35):** `SUBR('-NES',...,'')` counts months that hold 0 but not blank months, so the average depends on how the monthly update writes empty months. DH1 counts only non-zero months.
- **January window (8–9):** in early January MON = 12 uses all 12 values of attr 17. Whether attr 17 still holds last year's data then depends on the year-end roll, which is not in this program.
- **Filter multiplier (55):** `QUAN*(12-MOS)` multiplies a quantity, not a rate. With MOS = 1 a 20 becomes 220 (capped to 50). Probably meant something else.
- **No MOS validation (11):** non-numeric MOS is treated as 0, so every product falls under the 10-unit minimum and nothing is written, silently.
- **ORDER not cleared (68):** results accumulate with earlier IV21/DH1 runs. `IV.ORD.REC` option 2 then puts the whole file on one PO for one vendor (IV.ORD.REC.09).
- **READU/MATREADU silent-fallthrough:** not present (no update locks used).

## 8. Schema-design notes for the CTO

- Entities: Suggested Order Line (product, qty, run, buyer, parameters), Product Monthly Sales History (this year / last year by calendar month), Product Availability (on hand, on order, member demand).
- Three competing suggestion formulas exist: IV21 (seasonal projection with 2× growth cap), DH1 (seasonal with uncapped growth, rounding to attr 2) and the IVMST dictionary I-descriptors `MIN.STK` = INT(YTD.ORD / 10 × MOS), minimum 1, and `Order` = MIN.STK − TOT.AVAIL rounded to `CASE`, where TOT.AVAIL = STOCK + ORDERED − REQ and ORDERED = SUM(attr 21) + SUM(attr 23). The business must pick one before PIV2 builds a suggestion engine.
- IVMST has fields neither program uses: 34 `MOS` (months of supply per product), 61 `MIN.STK`, 16 `MOQ`, 27 `CASE`, 33 `CARTON`, 37 `A.VENDOR`, 46 `HOLD`. PIV2 should decide which are the real per-product parameters.
- Monthly history is stored as two 12-slot arrays that roll each year. PIV2 should derive monthly sales from invoice lines (SCANORD) by date, which removes the 20th-of-month and year-roll questions.
- On order must be derived from open PO lines (ordered − received − cancelled), not from IVMST 21.
- Suggestions should be grouped by vendor (IVMST 37 A.VENDOR) so they can become one PO per vendor.
- Missing audit: who ran the suggestion, with which parameters, and which quantities the buyer changed.
- Extract before schema commit: `DH1` (done), IVMST dictionary items `MIN.STK`, `Order`, `TOT.AVAIL`, the monthly update that fills attr 17/18 (`IV.UPD.SCANORD`?), `IV.AUTO.ORDER` (done).
