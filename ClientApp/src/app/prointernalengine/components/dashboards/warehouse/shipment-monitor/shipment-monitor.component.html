What is an Alert?
Alerts watch your shipments and show you when something needs attention.
How to Create One
  • Name your alert
  • (Optional) Enter an account
  • Select a zone (distance)
  • Select an SLA status
  • Set minimum days in transit
What “Minimum Days” Means
Don’t alert until the shipment has been moving this many days.
Example
Zone 8 + OnTime + 3 days
→ Shows long-distance shipments that have been moving 3+ days
Notes
  • Transit starts when UPS receives the package
  • Zones 7–8 take longer
  • Holiday rules may affect delivery timing
